About Me

Aspiring software developer with a passion for learning and building cool things with code.

I'm currently Majoring in Computer Science at Norfolk State University as a proud DNIMAS Scholar, and I'm especially interested in programming and how languages can be used to create software. I've earned my IT+ and Net+ certifications, and completed a two-year dual enrollment cybersecurity program through Tidewater Community College and the Chesapeake Career Center — where I also earned a Career Studies Certificate.

I'm open to opportunities in programming or cybersecurity, and I'm always excited to learn something new, grow my skills, and contribute wherever I can.

I started making text-based games with Twinery just for fun, and along the way, I taught myself HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. That's when I discovered my passion for bringing stories to life through code.
